# MISUKI PAINT EXECUTION PROTOCOL V4

Machine-first protocol. It is not a human tutorial and it must not be reduced to vague prose.

## Absolute rule

A stage cannot advance if its structural validation fails. Repair the current stage before drawing later detail.

## Character pipeline

1. P01 — reserve safe canvas margins; draw center axis, face construction and eye guide.
2. P02 — build smooth head contour; place two horn axes. Validate head curvature and horn roots.
3. P03 — draw and fill large rear-hair masses. No parallel-stripe hair. Protect the face window.
4. P04 — construct eyes, iris, brows, minimal nose and small mouth from guides.
5. P05 — connect neck, choker and sloped shoulders.
6. P06 — create curved gothic torso/outfit silhouette, then neckline and only a few high-value details.
7. P07 — expand horn axes into tapered volumes; add sparse ridges and a separation rim.
8. P08 — build 3-7 filled fringe locks; add 1-2 side locks and <=16 selective internal strands.
9. P09 — commit black masses while preserving face negative space.
10. P10 — add sparse gray separation and test thumbnail tonal hierarchy.
11. P11 — add lashes/lace/jewel, then erase construction guides.
12. P12 — thumbnail identity gate. If horns+hair+face do not read immediately, repair.
13. P13 — structural fidelity gate >=82 and no critical failure.
14. P14 — export only approved output plus checkpoints and paint plan.

## Scenario pipeline

S01 composition grid -> S02 background mass -> S03 architecture/nature -> S04 lighting -> S05 props -> S06 atmosphere -> S07 foreground frame -> S08 scene gate.

Scene gate requirements:
- face is first focal point;
- horns remain readable;
- background detail is reduced behind face;
- there are distinct foreground/midground/background planes;
- one dominant light direction is visible;
- props do not cut through face or horn silhouette.

## Repair order

silhouette > horns > hair > face > outfit > tone > detail.

## Hard negative rules

- no roof/pentagon hair top;
- no comb-like hair stripes;
- no rectangular torso;
- no horizontal outfit placeholder bars;
- no detached horn roots;
- no equal line weight everywhere;
- no micro-detail before silhouette is approved;
- no busy high-contrast object behind the face;
- no export after failed P12/P13.
